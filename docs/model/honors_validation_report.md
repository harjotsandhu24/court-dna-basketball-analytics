## Sources and snapshots

- `nba_com_finals_mvp_history`: NBA.com, <https://www.nba.com/news/history-finals-mvp-winners>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Finals MVP by season, 1996-97 to 2025-26, with team names.
- `bbref_finals_mvp`: Basketball-Reference, <https://www.basketball-reference.com/awards/finals_mvp.html>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Finals MVP with player ids and team abbreviations.
- `nba_com_pr_2025_26_awards`: NBA Communications, <https://pr.nba.com/voting-results-2025-26-nba-regular-season-awards/>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Official 2025-26 regular-season award winners and All-NBA, All-Defensive and All-Rookie teams. Winners only; voting results are not used.
- `bbref_awards_2026`: Basketball-Reference, <https://www.basketball-reference.com/awards/awards_2026.html>, retrieved 2026-10-16. MVP and Rookie of the Year plus the three team awards, with ids. Its Defensive Player, Sixth Man and Most Improved sections were empty when fetched.
- `wikipedia_2025_26_nba_season`: Wikipedia, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NBA_season>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Secondary source for the five individual awards, the champion and Finals result.
- `nba_com_season_recaps`: NBA.com, <https://www.nba.com/news/history-season-recaps-index>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Champion and Finals result for every season.
- `bbref_playoffs_2026`: Basketball-Reference, <https://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/NBA_2026.html>, retrieved 2026-10-16. 2026 playoff series results and champion.
- `bbref_team_page_nyk_2026`: Basketball-Reference, <https://www.basketball-reference.com/teams/NYK/2026.html>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Knicks 2025-26 season page: playoff record 16-3 and the title.
- `wikipedia_2026_nba_finals`: Wikipedia, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NBA_Finals>, retrieved 2026-10-16. 2026 Finals result. It lists players who appeared in the series, not a ring or roster list.
- `wikipedia_playoffs_and_season_pages`: Wikipedia, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_NBA_playoffs>, retrieved 2026-10-16. Playoff seeds from each season's playoff page (2003, 2009-2014, 2016-2026) or the standings table on the season page (1997-2002, 2004-2008, 2015). Published seeds are not always record order (for example division winners in 2004-2015). A secondary source, not the NBA's own media guide.
- `kaggle_nba_stats`: Kaggle (Sumit Sodatta), compiled from Basketball-Reference, <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sumitrodatta/nba-aba-baa-stats>, retrieved 2026-10-10. All-Star selections and the awards for 1996-97 to 2024-25. Single source.
